## Past-paper question index

These are pointers to questions in six past examination papers. Question wording is not reproduced, and some figures and equations were not legible in the source copies.

| Paper | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Mid 252 · Final 252 | Midterm and final, trimester 252 (Summer 2025) |
| Mid 253 · Final 253 | Midterm and final, trimester 253 (Fall 2025) |
| Mid 261 · Final 261 | Midterm and final, trimester 261 (Spring 2026) |

For example, "Final 253 Q2c" is the final examination of trimester 253, question 2, part (c).

**Vectors.** Mid 252 Q1c, Q2a, Q2b; Mid 253 Q1b, Q2a; Mid 261 Q1c, Q2a, Q2b, Q5a, Q5b.

**Kinematics and projectile motion.** Constant-velocity motion in two dimensions: Mid 252 Q2c; Mid 253 Q2b; Mid 261 Q3b. Projectiles: Mid 252 Q5a; Mid 253 Q3b, Q4a; Mid 261 Q1b, Q3a, Q4a.

**Newton's laws.** No past-paper question examines this directly.

**Circular motion.** Mid 252 Q1a, Q3b, Q5b; Mid 253 Q4b; Mid 261 Q1a, Q3c, Q4b.

**Work, energy and torque.** Mid 261 Q2c.

**Fluid mechanics.** Mid 252 Q1b, Q3a, Q4b; Mid 253 Q1a, Q1c, Q3a, Q5a, Q5b; Final 261 Q3c, Q5b.

**Simple harmonic motion.** Mid 252 Q3c, Q4a; Final 252 Q1a, Q2a, Q4b; Final 253 Q1a, Q1c, Q2a, Q2b, Q2c, Q4b, Q5a, Q5b(i); Final 261 Q1a, Q2a, Q2b, Q2c, Q4a, Q4b.

**Damped oscillations.** Final 252 Q1b, Q2c, Q4a; Final 253 Q1b, Q3a, Q3b, Q4a; Final 261 Q1b, Q1c, Q3a, Q5a.

**LC and RLC circuits.** Final 252 Q2b; Final 253 Q3c, Q5b(ii); Final 261 Q3b.

**Waves and interference (provisional).** Final 252 Q1c, Q3a, Q3b, Q3c, Q5a, Q5b. Neither Final 253 nor Final 261 has a wave or interference question.

**Elasticity, surface tension, terminal velocity (unresolved).** No past-paper question examines these.

The six papers are a limited sample. A topic's absence from them does not mean it is absent from the course.
