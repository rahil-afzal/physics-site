## Learning Goals

- Compute damped-oscillator quantities.
- Sketch and interpret the three regimes and the zero-damping limit.

## The underdamped solution

$$x=A_0e^{-\gamma t}\sin(\omega' t+\varphi),\qquad \omega'=\sqrt{\omega_0^2-\gamma^2}=\sqrt{\frac km-\frac{b^2}{4m^2}}$$

The solution is stated and verified by substitution, not derived in full.

## Amplitude envelope and half-amplitude time

$$A(t)=A_0e^{-\gamma t}=A_0e^{-bt/2m},\qquad t_{1/2}=\frac{\ln2}{\gamma}=\frac{2m\ln2}{b}$$

*(to be written)*

## Lifetime

$$\tau=\frac1\gamma=\frac{2m}{b}\quad\text{(amplitude e-fold)}$$

## Energy decay

$$E\propto e^{-2\gamma t}=e^{-bt/m}\quad\text{(weak damping)}$$

## Sketches of the three regimes

Damping lowers the oscillation frequency and shrinks the amplitude exponentially; the zero-damping limit recovers SHM.
