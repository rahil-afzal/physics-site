## Learning Goals

- Compute dot and cross products.
- Find the angle between two vectors.
- Find the vector between two points, its length, and its angle with an axis.

## Dot product

$$\vec A\cdot\vec B = AB\cos\theta = A_xB_x+A_yB_y+A_zB_z$$

### Derivation: component form

From $\hat i\cdot\hat i=1$, $\hat i\cdot\hat j=0$, and so on.

*(to be written)*

## Cross product

$$\lvert\vec A\times\vec B\rvert = AB\sin\theta,\qquad \vec A\times\vec B=\begin{vmatrix}\hat i&\hat j&\hat k\\A_x&A_y&A_z\\B_x&B_y&B_z\end{vmatrix}$$

The direction follows the right-hand rule.

### Derivation: determinant form

Stated and justified from the unit-vector cross products.

## Angle between two vectors

$$\cos\theta=\frac{\vec A\cdot\vec B}{AB}$$

## Vector between two points

$$\overrightarrow{PQ}=\vec r_Q-\vec r_P$$
