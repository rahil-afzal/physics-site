## Components and unit vectors

$$\vec A = A_x\hat i + A_y\hat j + A_z\hat k,\qquad A=\sqrt{A_x^2+A_y^2+A_z^2}$$

$$A_x = A\cos\theta,\quad A_y = A\sin\theta,\quad \theta=\tan^{-1}\frac{A_y}{A_x}$$

## Resultant of two equal forces

$$R=2F\cos\frac{\theta}{2},\qquad R^2=2F^2(1+\cos\theta)=4F^2\cos^2\frac{\theta}{2}$$

This gives $R=2F$ at $0^\circ$, $\sqrt2\,F$ at $90^\circ$, and $0$ at $180^\circ$.
