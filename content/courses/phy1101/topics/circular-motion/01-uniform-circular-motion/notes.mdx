## Centripetal acceleration and force

$$a_c=\frac{v^2}{r}=\omega^2 r,\qquad F_c=\frac{mv^2}{r}$$

Centripetal force is the net inward force, not a new force. The relation $a_c=\omega^2 r$ is reused in SHM (Class 13).

### Derivation

$$\vec r=R(\cos\omega t\,\hat i+\sin\omega t\,\hat j)\ \Rightarrow\ \vec v=\dot{\vec r},\quad \vec a=\dot{\vec v}=-\omega^2\vec r$$
