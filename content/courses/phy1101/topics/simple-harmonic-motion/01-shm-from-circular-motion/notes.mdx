## Learning Goals

- Derive SHM kinematics from circular motion.
- Read amplitude, period and phase from a graph.
- Sketch the displacement, velocity and acceleration graphs and relate their phases.

<Callout type="note" title="Convention">
A particle starts on the positive $x$-axis and moves counterclockwise on a circle of radius $A$ with angular speed $\omega$. Its vertical projection is $y=A\sin\omega t$.
</Callout>

$$y=A\sin\omega t$$

## Velocity and acceleration

$$v_y=\omega A\cos\omega t,\qquad a_y=-\omega^2A\sin\omega t=-\omega^2y,\qquad v=\pm\omega\sqrt{A^2-y^2}$$

### Derivations from the projection

- Position: the projection of the position vector gives $y=A\sin\omega t$.
- Velocity: the tangential velocity has magnitude $\omega A$; its vertical component is $\omega A\cos\omega t$.
- Acceleration: the centripetal acceleration has magnitude $\omega^2A$; its vertical component is $-\omega^2A\sin\omega t$.
- Check by differentiation, and $v^2=\omega^2A^2\cos^2\omega t=\omega^2(A^2-y^2)$.

*(to be written)*

## Period, frequency and phase

$$T=\frac{2\pi}{\omega},\qquad f=\frac1T,\qquad y=A\sin(\omega t+\varphi),\qquad v_{\max}=\omega A,\qquad a_{\max}=\omega^2A$$
