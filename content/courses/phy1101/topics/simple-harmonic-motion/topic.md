**Prerequisites:** Circular Motion (U4); Newton's Laws (U3); energy (U5). **Leads to:** Damped Oscillations (U8), LC and RLC Circuits (U9), Waves (U10). The physical derivation and the sinusoidal solution come first; no advanced differential-equation methods are used (Revision 2 decision).
