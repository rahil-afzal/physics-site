## Bernoulli's equation

$$p+\tfrac12\rho v^2+\rho gh=\text{const}$$

Bernoulli's equation is energy conservation per unit volume. At a narrower section the speed is higher and the pressure lower.

### Derivation: work-energy theorem on a fluid element

For a fluid element of volume $\Delta V$ moving between two sections:

- work done by pressure: $(p_1-p_2)\Delta V$
- change in kinetic energy: $\Delta K=\tfrac12\rho\,\Delta V\,(v_2^2-v_1^2)$
- change in potential energy: $\Delta U=\rho\,\Delta V\,g\,(h_2-h_1)$

## Venturimeter

The constriction lowers the pressure; the measured pressure difference gives the speed and hence the flow rate.

<Figure>Figure to be supplied: a Venturimeter with the two pressure tappings</Figure>

$$Q=A_1v_1=A_1A_2\sqrt{\frac{2(p_1-p_2)}{\rho\,(A_1^2-A_2^2)}}$$

### Derivation: Venturimeter flow rate

From $p_1-p_2=\tfrac12\rho(v_2^2-v_1^2)$ and $v_2=v_1A_1/A_2$:

$$\Delta p=\tfrac12\rho v_1^2\left(\frac{A_1^2}{A_2^2}-1\right)$$

hence $v_1$ and $Q$.
