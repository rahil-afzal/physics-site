## Flow rate and continuity

$$Q=Av,\qquad A_1v_1=A_2v_2$$

A narrower pipe carries faster flow.

### Derivation: conservation of mass

$$\rho A_1v_1\Delta t=\rho A_2v_2\Delta t$$
