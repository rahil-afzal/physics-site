## Viscosity

Viscosity is internal friction in a fluid. Its coefficient is $\eta$, with SI unit Pa·s = N·s/m².

## Reynolds number

$$\mathrm{Re}=\frac{\rho vD}{\eta}$$

The Reynolds number is the ratio of inertial to viscous effects: low values correspond to smooth laminar flow, high values to turbulence.

<Callout type="warning" title="Provisional">
The Reynolds-number thresholds used by the department are yet to be confirmed. Conventional pipe-flow guide values are approximately $\mathrm{Re}<2000$ for laminar and $\mathrm{Re}>4000$ for turbulent flow.
</Callout>

### Dimensional check

$$\left[\frac{\rho vD}{\eta}\right]=\frac{(\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}})(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}})(\mathrm m)}{\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}\,s^{-1}}}=1$$
