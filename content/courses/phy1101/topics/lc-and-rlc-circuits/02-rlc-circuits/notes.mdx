## Learning Goals

- Derive the RLC differential equation.

- Compute the damped frequency and the critical resistance.

## The series RLC equation

$$L\ddot q+R\dot q+\frac qC=0\ \Rightarrow\ \ddot q+2\gamma\dot q+\omega_0^2q=0,\qquad \gamma=\frac{R}{2L},\quad \omega_0^2=\frac1{LC}$$

## Damping regimes

The resistor plays the role of the damper.

$$\omega'=\sqrt{\frac1{LC}-\frac{R^2}{4L^2}},\qquad f'=\frac{\omega'}{2\pi}$$

$$R<2\sqrt{\frac LC}\ \text{(oscillatory)},\qquad R=2\sqrt{\frac LC}\ \text{(critical)},\qquad R>2\sqrt{\frac LC}\ \text{(overdamped)}$$

## Mechanical-electrical analogy

| Mechanical | Electrical |
|---|---|
| $x$ | $q$ |
| $v=\dot x$ | $I=\dot q$ |
| $m$ | $L$ |
| $k$ | $1/C$ |
| $b$ | $R$ |
| $\tfrac12kx^2$ | $q^2/2C$ |
| $\tfrac12mv^2$ | $\tfrac12LI^2$ |
