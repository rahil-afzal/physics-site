## Capacitor, inductor and Kirchhoff's loop rule

A short primer, since no circuit knowledge is assumed.

$$q=CV,\quad U_C=\frac{q^2}{2C},\qquad \varepsilon_L=-L\frac{dI}{dt},\quad U_L=\tfrac12LI^2,\qquad I=\frac{dq}{dt}$$

*(to be written)*

## The LC equation

$$L\frac{dI}{dt}+\frac qC=0\ \Rightarrow\ \ddot q+\frac1{LC}q=0,\qquad \omega_0=\frac1{\sqrt{LC}},\qquad f=\frac1{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}$$

The solution is $q=Q_0\sin(\omega_0t+\varphi)$.

## Energy exchange

The capacitor stores energy in its electric field and the inductor in its magnetic field; the energy oscillates between them as kinetic and potential energy do in SHM.

$$U_C+U_L=\frac{Q_0^2}{2C}=\tfrac12LI_0^2,\qquad I_0=\omega_0Q_0$$
