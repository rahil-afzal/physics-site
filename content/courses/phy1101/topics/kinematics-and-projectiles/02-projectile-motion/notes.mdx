## Independence of horizontal and vertical motion

The horizontal component of velocity is constant; the vertical component changes at rate $g$.

$$x=v_0\cos\theta\,t,\qquad y=v_0\sin\theta\,t-\tfrac12 gt^2$$

$$v_x=v_0\cos\theta,\qquad v_y=v_0\sin\theta-gt$$

## Time of flight, maximum height and range

$$T=\frac{2v_0\sin\theta}{g},\qquad H=\frac{v_0^2\sin^2\theta}{2g},\qquad R=\frac{v_0^2\sin2\theta}{g}$$

## Maximum range and complementary angles

$$R_{\max}=\frac{v_0^2}{g}\ (\theta=45^\circ),\qquad R(\theta)=R(90^\circ-\theta)$$
